About The Club

Small Club. Big Curiosity


The FGTSC Science Club was founded in 2026 by a group of passionate students who believed that science isn't just a subject — it's a way of thinking, questioning and making a difference. What started as a small group of curious minds has now grown into a vibrant community of learners, creators and problem-solvers.

Our club provides a platform for students to explore scientific ideas, work on real-world projects, attend workshops, and build skills that go beyond the classroom. Whether you're interested in experiments, technology, nature, or space — there's a place for you here.
